Our Chapels & Kurisumthotty

Our parish is blessed with several sacred chapels that serve as beacons of faith and places of spiritual nourishment for our community.

Main Chapel

St. Mary's Chapel

Dedicated to St. Mary, the Mother of God, this chapel serves as a serene space for intimate worship and prayer. It stands as a testament to our community's devotion and is the heart of our Saturday morning liturgical life.

Regular Service: 2nd Saturday: 7:00 AM
Sacred Shrine

Mar Gregorius Kurisumthotty

A peaceful sanctuary honoring Mar Gregorius of Parumala (Parumala Thirumeni), the first canonized saint of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church. This Kurisumthotty provides a quiet retreat for personal reflection, where many find spiritual solace and strength.

Observances: Special occasions & Feast days
Parish Landmark

Mar Baselios Kurisumthotty

Dedicated in honor of Mar Baselios, this sacred Kurisumthotty is a cherished spiritual landmark of our parish, serving as a gathering point for liturgical processions, prayer, and festival observances throughout the ecclesiastical year.

Service: Festival services & Processions
