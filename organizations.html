Our Spiritual Organizations

Our church is blessed with vibrant spiritual organizations that nurture faith, foster community, and serve others in Christ's name.

Vanitha Samajam

The Vanitha Samajam (Women's Association) is a dedicated group of women who serve as the backbone of our church community. Through prayer, fellowship, and service, they embody the virtues of faith, charity, and devotion. The association organizes spiritual retreats, charitable activities, and supports various church initiatives.

Activities Include:
  • Monthly prayer meetings and Bible study
  • Charitable works and community service
  • Spiritual retreats and workshops
  • Cultural programs and celebrations

Sunday School

Our Sunday School program provides spiritual education and moral guidance to children and youth. Through engaging lessons, hymns, and activities, we help young minds develop a strong foundation in Christian faith and values. The program runs every Sunday, nurturing the next generation of faithful believers.

Activities Include:
  • Bible stories and teachings
  • Hymn singing and worship
  • Craft and educational activities
  • Youth camps and outings

Youth Association

The Youth Association engages young adults in spiritual growth, leadership development, and community service. Through various programs and activities, we help youth discover their faith, build meaningful relationships, and contribute positively to society. The association serves as a bridge between tradition and modernity.

Activities Include:
  • Youth fellowship and prayer meetings
  • Leadership training programs
  • Community service projects
  • Sports and recreational activities

Family Units

Family Units bring together families within our parish to strengthen bonds, share faith, and support one another. These groups organize family-oriented activities, prayer meetings, and fellowship events that help families grow spiritually together. Each unit serves as a small community within our larger church family.

Activities Include:
  • Family prayer meetings
  • Family-oriented celebrations
  • Mutual support and fellowship
  • Family camps and retreats

Church Choir

The Church Choir elevates our worship through sacred liturgical music, lead singing during Holy Qurbana, and traditional Syriac hymns. Dedicated choristers lead the congregation in prayerful harmony, preserving ancient liturgical music traditions while praising God.

Activities Include:
  • Leading liturgical singing during Holy Qurbana
  • Choir practice and vocal training
  • Special feast day and Christmas Carol performances
  • Preserving traditional Syriac and Malayalam hymns
