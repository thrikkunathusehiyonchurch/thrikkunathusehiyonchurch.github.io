Welcome to Our Parish

A Legacy of Faith & Community

Welcome to Thrikkunathu Sehiyon Jacobite Syrian Church, a vibrant community of faith rooted in the ancient traditions of the Universal Syrian Orthodox Church. Located in Kunnackal, our parish serves as a sacred spiritual home for families seeking to deepen their relationship with God through worship, fellowship, and compassionate service.

We are committed to preserving our rich apostolic heritage while nurturing future generations. Our congregation is dedicated to living out the teachings of Christ, fostering a warm and inclusive sanctuary for spiritual growth.

Building Community

We strive to build a united, faith-filled community where relationships are strengthened and spiritual growth is encouraged.

Our History & Heritage

Thrikkunathu Sehiyon Jacobite Syrian Church Kunnackal stands as a testament to the enduring faith and spiritual resilience of the Syrian Christian community in the Muvattupuzha region.

Origins and Early Years

The church’s journey began in the early 20th century, specifically in the year 1932 (1107 of the Malayalam Era). During this period, the local Nasrani families in Kunnackal and Kadathy felt a profound need for a dedicated place of worship that remained steadfast under the Holy Apostolic See of Antioch.

Through collective prayer and unwavering devotion, the community secured the necessary approvals to establish the parish on a hillock in Mulavoor, naming it in honor of Mar Geevarghese Sahada (St. George).

Ecclesiastical Heritage

From its inception, the church has been deeply rooted in the ancient traditions of the Universal Syrian Orthodox Church. Its establishment allowed the community to construct the church and conduct liturgical services according to the rich heritage of the Syrian Malabar Nasranis.

Original Church Building (1932)

Looking Forward

Today, Thrikkunathu Sehiyon Jacobite Syrian Church Kunnackal remains a thriving pillar of community. With our newly consecrated sanctuary, we continue our mission of spiritual guidance, charitable works, and the preservation of our apostolic faith for generations to come.
